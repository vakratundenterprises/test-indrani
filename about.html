Established in 2000, the Group has grown under the visionary leadership of Mr. Prashant Patil and Mr. Gurudatta Patil, laying a strong foundation of trust, excellence, and business integrity. Carrying this legacy forward, the next generation, Mr. Harshvardhan Patil and Mr. Jayvardhan Patil, combines the Group's rich experience with fresh perspectives, modern leadership, and a forward-thinking vision.

Today, with five successful companies and a workforce of over 250 professionals, the Group continues to expand its presence and strengthen its position across industries. Recognized with several prestigious awards for consistent performance, the Group remains committed to excellence, innovation, and sustainable growth.
